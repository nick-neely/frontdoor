# nickneely.dev

The personal brand site for Nick Neely: one canonical hub that consolidates consulting, shipped work, and writing under a single identity. Language here is deliberately narrower than the code that implements it, so that a page, a route, a frontmatter field, and an issue title all use the same word for the same thing.

## Language

### Work and identity

**Role**: A period of employment or founding, with an employer, a title, and dates. Roles form the timeline on the work page. _Avoid_: job, position, stint

**Engagement**: A consulting arrangement between Nick and a client, contracted through Neely Solutions. _Avoid_: project, contract, gig

**Proof Point**: A single quantified outcome from a Role or an Engagement, presented as one scannable row of context and result, dated by its own year or by the Role that carries it. Its figure is verbatim from documented work or it is absent. _Avoid_: achievement, highlight, win, metric

**Neely Solutions**: The separate business brand serving local and small-business clients. It is linked from this site and never co-headlined on it. _Avoid_: the agency, my company, NS

**Now Line**: The strip beneath the bio stating current context: what Nick is building, where he consults, and where he lives. A live push, when there is one, hangs on the next line rather than extending the sentence. _Avoid_: status, tagline, bio line

### Things that were built

**Project**: Anything shipped that has a name, a year, and a link. Every Project is exactly one kind: Product, Client Work, or Experiment. _Avoid_: portfolio piece, work sample, build

**Product**: A Project offered to people generally, independent of any single client. _Avoid_: app, tool, SaaS, side project

**Client Work**: A Project delivered for a paying client, usually through Neely Solutions. Naming the client requires written sign-off; without it the Project ships described but unnamed. _Avoid_: freelance job, contract work, gig

**Experiment**: A Project built to learn or for fun, with no client and no audience obligation. _Avoid_: toy, side project, hobby project

**Status**: Where a Project sits in its lifecycle: `Active`, `Shipped`, `Archived`, or `Hiatus`. Every Project carries exactly one. _Avoid_: state, stage, phase

### Writing

**Post**: A dated piece of writing, tied to exactly one Pillar. _Avoid_: article, blog, blog post, entry

**Note**: An undated, evergreen piece of writing meant to be revised rather than superseded. _Avoid_: evergreen post, wiki page, reference doc

**Pillar**: One of three subject areas every Post belongs to: product engineering, practical AI, or building in public. A piece that fits no Pillar does not belong on this site. _Avoid_: category, topic, section, series

**Tag**: A free-form secondary label on a Post. Tags organize; only a Pillar classifies. _Avoid_: keyword, label

### Shared surfaces

**Update**: A dated, linkable thing that happened: a published Post or a Project milestone. Updates from every source merge into one feed on the home page. _Avoid_: news, activity, changelog, feed item

**Subscriber**: A person who asked for the newsletter and confirmed that request by email. Someone who submitted the form but has not confirmed is not a Subscriber. _Avoid_: user, member, lead, contact

**Confirmation**: The signed proof that a person asked for the newsletter at a specific moment. It travels in the confirm link and is never stored. _Avoid_: token, verification, opt-in record
