# /work shows the career, and Neely Solutions owns the consulting funnel

`/work` is a résumé-style record for people evaluating Nick - where he has worked, what each role measurably produced, and how he works - in the spirit of ambrosino.io's timeline. The consulting funnel that page originally carried moves to Neely Solutions, which is where consulting is actually contracted through his LLC. The page routes consulting seekers there in one quiet line near the top and again in a fuller block, carrying Jared Gringer's testimonial, at the bottom.

The cal.com link survives, reframed: it is a general "talk to me" - a role, AI-assisted development, whatever the visitor is building - and explicitly not the consulting door. The stance is passive-confident: no open-to-work language, because Nick is employed and the work speaks for itself.

## Considered options

**Keeping the consulting one-pager** was the shipped v1 and the original issue's design. Rejected because it duplicated Neely Solutions' actual job and split one funnel across two sites. The personal site's differentiator is the person - the career, the products, the writing - not a second contract-intake surface.

**A hybrid page serving both audiences equally** was rejected as the worst of both: a hiring manager reads a sales pitch, a consulting buyer reads a résumé, and neither feels addressed.

## Consequences

PRODUCT.md's success statement changes: an engineering leader finds the proof and reaches out, rather than books a call. CONTEXT.md's Proof Point widens to quantified outcomes from any Role or Engagement, since the timeline now carries employment outcomes alongside consulting ones.

Quantified claims on the page come verbatim from Nick's résumé (2026-07-26 revision), which is his documented record. The Frontline Fuel waitlist and first-month revenue figures carry the client's explicit sign-off, obtained for that résumé.

Reversal is cheap in code and expensive in narrative: the page is public under his name, and flipping between "hire my company" and "here is my career" repeatedly would read as indecision. This decision is expected to hold.
