This page exists so the Note half of the writing surface can be looked at. Open
it in both themes and read the line under the title.

## What this fixture proves

The metadata line above carries no publication date. A Note is revised rather
than superseded, so the day it was first written is provenance rather than
news; what it can honestly report is the day it was last touched, and it
reports that because this file's frontmatter records an `updated`. Remove that
field and the line should fall back to the reading time and the Pillar alone.

Everything after the date reads exactly as it does on a Post. The two kinds
share one prose surface, and `writing-surface-fixture.mdx` is where every part
of that surface is exercised; nothing here repeats it.

## What it proves by being absent

Set `draft: false`, restart nothing, and `/writing` grows a second list: a
"Posts" heading over the dated feed, a "Notes" heading over this, sorted by
title and printing no dates. Revert it and the page returns to the single
undivided list it ships with.

While it is published, `/rss.xml` must still not mention it, and neither must
the Latest feed on the home page. Both are lists of dated things that happened,
and a Note is neither dated nor a thing that happened once.
